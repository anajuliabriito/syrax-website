Questions

Does anyone need to fly the drone?

No. The dock launches, lands and charges it on schedule.

Do I need a drone licence?

No. We handle the operating requirements as part of setup.

Why do flights run mid-morning?

Short shadows. Cattle under trees are easiest to see, so counts are most reliable.

Will the drone stress my cattle?

It flies well above the herd on the same route every day. Cattle get used to it quickly.

What does my property need?

Power at the dock and a mobile or satellite connection. We check both in the assessment.

What does it cost?

It depends on your property. You get a fixed quote after the assessment.
