How Syrax works.

A dock on your property, a drone on a schedule, and software that tells you what changed.

Get Syrax on your property

Three parts. No pilot. No one on site.

The dock

Lives on your property. Launches, lands and charges the drone, in any season.

The drone

Visual and thermal cameras. Flies the same mapped route every day.

The software

Turns each flight into a count and tells you what changed.

From first call to first count

  1. 01

    Assess

    We check coverage, power and where the dock should go.

  2. 02

    Install

    The dock goes in and every route is mapped.

  3. 03

    Fly and count

    The drone launches on schedule, flies the route and returns to charge.

  4. 04

    Act

    You get a text with what changed and the full report in the app.

When it flies

Counting flights run mid-morning. Shadows are short and cattle under trees are easier to see, so that's when a count is most reliable.

The dock waits out unsafe weather and flies when conditions allow. You'll always know when the next flight is due.
