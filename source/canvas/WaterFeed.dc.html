Water troughs

A trough can stop filling half an hour after the water run. Syrax checks it every day instead of every week.

  • Every trough on the route photographed
  • Low or empty troughs flagged straight away

Feed bunks

The mapping flight measures what's left in each bunk, so you know when to top up without driving out.

  • Remaining feed estimated from the mapping flight
  • Top-up alerts before bunks run empty
  • Feed use tracked over time

Already using water telemetry?

Keep it. Telemetry tells you about one tank. Syrax sees every trough the drone passes, plus your cattle, feed and fences, on one flight.

Questions

Does Syrax replace tank sensors?

It complements them. Sensors watch one point continuously. Syrax checks every trough on the route once a day and adds your cattle, feed and fences.

How accurate are feed estimates?

Bunk volumes are estimated from mapping imagery. They're designed to tell you when to top up, not to replace a weighbridge.
