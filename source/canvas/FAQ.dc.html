Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers on setup, the drone, counting accuracy and cost.

Get Syrax on your property

Getting started

What does setup involve?

A property assessment, installing the dock and mapping your routes. We handle all of it.

What does my property need?

Power at the dock site and a mobile or satellite connection. We check both in the assessment.

Can I use a drone I already own?

Syrax is built around the DJI Dock 3. If you already have DJI enterprise equipment, we'll check whether it can be used.

The drone and dock

Does anyone need to fly the drone?

No. The dock launches, lands and charges it on schedule.

Do I need a drone licence?

No. We handle the operating requirements as part of setup.

Will the drone stress my cattle?

It flies well above the herd on the same route every day. Cattle get used to it quickly.

What happens in bad weather?

The dock waits out unsafe conditions and flies when it's safe.

Counting and accuracy

Why do flights run mid-morning?

Short shadows. Cattle under trees are easiest to see, so counts are most reliable.

How do I know a count is right?

Every report shows the photo with every animal marked, and compares it with the previous flight.

Does it identify individual animals?

Not yet. Syrax counts head by paddock and flags changes.

Data and cost

Who can see my data?

Only the people you give access to. Every account uses secure sign-in.

What does it cost?

It depends on your property. You get a fixed quote after the assessment.
