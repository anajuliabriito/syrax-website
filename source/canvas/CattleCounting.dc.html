Questions

Can it count cattle under trees?

Mid-morning flights and a thermal camera find most animals in cover. Shade can still hide some, which is why every count is checked against yesterday and backed by photos.

Does it identify individual animals?

Not yet. Syrax counts head by paddock and flags changes. Individual identification is on our roadmap.

Can I share counts with my accountant or lender?

Yes. Every report is dated and photo-backed, and can be shared as a link or download.
